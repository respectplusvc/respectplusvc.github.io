Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Respect+ Uploader is a private tool used only by Respect+ to publish its own videos to its own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

What we access

When the account owner authorises the tool, it receives access tokens from those platforms that allow it to upload videos and read basic statistics (such as view counts) for the Respect+ accounts. It does not access any other user's data.

How it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's connected-apps settings. Questions: respectplus.clips@gmail.com
